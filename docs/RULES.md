# Game rules (Card Rush – unofficial UNO-inspired)

## Standard rules (always on)

- 108-card deck per 1 deck: 0 (x1) and 1-9 (x2) in four colours, Skip/Reverse/Draw Two (x2 per colour), 4 Wild, 4 Wild Draw Four.
- 7 cards each. A random dealer is chosen; play starts on the dealer's left.
- **Starting card:** Wild Draw Four is put back and replaced. Skip skips the first player; Reverse flips direction and the dealer plays first; Draw Two makes the first player draw 2 and miss the turn; Wild lets the first player choose the colour.
- Play a card matching the top card's colour, number or symbol. Wilds are always playable.
- **No playable card (or you choose to draw):** draw one card. If it can be played you may play it, otherwise (or if you decline) the turn passes. Only the drawn card may be played after drawing.
- **Skip** – next player loses their turn. **Reverse** – direction flips (acts as Skip with 2 players). **Draw Two** – next player draws 2 and loses the turn. **Wild** – pick a colour.
- **Wild Draw Four** – pick a colour; the next player draws 4 and loses the turn. It is _legal_ only if the player holds no card of the previously active colour (other wilds and same-number cards do not count). It may be played illegally as a bluff. The victim may **challenge**:
  - challenge succeeds (it was illegal): the offender draws 4; the challenger keeps their turn and the chosen colour stands;
  - challenge fails: the challenger draws 6 and loses the turn.
- **UNO:** with one card left press UNO!. Pressing at two cards on your own turn pre-declares it. If you drop to one card without calling and another player presses _Catch_ **before the next player acts**, you draw 2.
- Empty discard/draw: the discard pile (minus the top card) is reshuffled into the draw pile.
- **Winning:** the first player to empty their hand wins the round and scores the value of the other hands (numbers face value, Skip/Reverse/Draw Two 20, Wild/Wild Draw Four 50). A final Draw Two/Wild Draw Four still makes the next player draw before scoring; the winning play ends the round immediately (no challenge of a final +4). Scores accumulate over rounds in a room.
- **Extended capacity (up to 20 players):** classic UNO is not officially designed for more than ~10 players. Tables where `players*7 + 40 > 108` automatically shuffle two decks together (2–9 players: 1 deck, 10+ players: 2 decks). Hand size stays 7.

## House rules (all off by default; chosen before the game and fixed for the round)

| Rule                                      | Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| +2 on +2 / +4 on +4 / +4 on +2 / +2 on +4 | Independent toggles. A victim of a draw card may answer with an allowed card instead of drawing; the pending penalty accumulates (+2 or +4 each) and passes to the next player. +2-on-+2 and +4 stacks ignore colour; +2-on-+4 must match the colour chosen by the +4. The victim can always instead draw the whole pending penalty and lose the turn. The victim always has to tap Draw to take the penalty (the turn visibly moves to them first). |
| Stacking and challenges                   | Only the **first** Wild Draw Four in a chain can be challenged (by its direct victim). Answering with a stack card forfeits the challenge; stacked +4s cannot be challenged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 7-0                                       | Playing 7 swaps hands with a chosen player; playing 0 passes every hand one seat in the direction of play.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Jump-In                                   | Any player may play a card identical (same colour _and_ symbol, coloured cards only) to the top card out of turn; play continues from the jumper. Not allowed while a penalty is pending, or after the current player has drawn.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Draw Until Playable                       | Drawing continues until a playable card is found.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Force Play                                | A playable drawn card is played automatically (a wild still asks for a colour).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Disconnections and removal

- Disconnected players stay in the game. If it is their turn the table **pauses** (nobody is skipped, no auto-play, no bot substitution). If it is someone else's turn play continues until the turn reaches them.
- The host may **remove a disconnected player** (confirmation required). Policy: their hand is placed, in order, at the bottom of the draw pile; any pending penalty/choice aimed at them is cancelled and the turn passes to the next player in the direction of play; if only one player remains they win. Removed (and voluntarily departed) credentials can never reconnect.
